# COVA Art Academy Website Design Guidelines

## Design Approach
**Reference-Based Approach** - Drawing inspiration from educational platforms like Coursera and creative portfolios like Behance, combined with the clean aesthetics of art gallery websites. The design should reflect artistic sophistication while maintaining educational clarity.

## Core Design Elements

### Color Palette
**Light Mode:**
- Primary: 25 15% 20% (Deep charcoal for sophistication)
- Secondary: 200 25% 95% (Light blue-gray for calm learning environment)
- Accent: 45 85% 60% (Warm orange for creative energy - use sparingly)
- Background: 0 0% 98% (Clean white base)

**Dark Mode:**
- Primary: 200 20% 85% (Soft light blue-gray)
- Secondary: 25 10% 15% (Deep warm gray)
- Accent: 45 75% 65% (Slightly muted warm orange)
- Background: 220 15% 8% (Rich dark background)

### Typography
- **Primary Font**: Inter (Google Fonts) - clean, readable for educational content
- **Accent Font**: Playfair Display (Google Fonts) - elegant serif for artistic touches in headings
- Hierarchy: 4xl/3xl for heroes, xl/lg for section headers, base/sm for body text

### Layout System
**Tailwind Spacing Units**: Consistently use 4, 8, 12, and 16 for margins, padding, and gaps
- 4 units for tight spacing between related elements
- 8 units for standard component spacing
- 12 units for section separations
- 16 units for major layout divisions

### Component Library
- **Navigation**: Clean horizontal nav with subtle underline animations
- **Cards**: Elevated cards with soft shadows for curriculum modules
- **Buttons**: Primary solid buttons and outline variants with proper blur backgrounds over images
- **Typography**: Strong hierarchy with artistic serif accents
- **Forms**: Minimal contact forms with soft focus states

## Visual Treatment & Gradients
- **Hero Section**: Subtle gradient overlay from 25 20% 15% to 200 15% 25% over background imagery
- **Section Backgrounds**: Alternating subtle gradients from 200 5% 97% to 45 8% 95%
- **Creative Accents**: Use warm orange gradient highlights (45 85% 60% to 35 90% 55%) sparingly for call-to-action elements

## Images
**Hero Image**: Large, inspiring image of students engaged in thoughtful art creation - should show both the process and contemplative nature of the COVA method
**Additional Images**: 
- Grade 10 section: Visual journal examples and process documentation
- Grade 11 section: High-quality student artwork and entrance exam preparation scenes
- Philosophy section: Abstract artistic imagery representing "thinking while drawing"

## Page Structure (Maximum 4 Sections)
1. **Hero Section**: COVA philosophy introduction with large background image
2. **Educational Philosophy**: "Thinking while drawing" core concept with visual examples  
3. **Curriculum Progression**: Grade 10 exploration → Grade 11 practical mastery
4. **Contact/Enrollment**: Simple, elegant call-to-action with academy information

## Key Design Principles
- **Artistic Sophistication**: Reflect the creative nature while maintaining professionalism
- **Educational Clarity**: Information hierarchy that guides parents and students naturally
- **Progressive Revelation**: Show the journey from exploration to mastery visually
- **Minimal Distractions**: Focus on content with strategic use of creative elements
